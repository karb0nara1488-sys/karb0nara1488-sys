## What I do

I create clear, considered presentation and document design for people and businesses that want their ideas to look as professional as they are.

| PRESENTATIONS | DOCUMENTS | VISUAL MATERIALS |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Pitch decks & business presentations | Proposals & reports | Branded layouts |
| Company profiles | Structured documents | Supporting graphics |
| Custom slide design | Polished business materials | Consistent visual systems |

## The approach

**01 — Understand**  
Clarify the goal, audience and key message.

**02 — Structure**  
Organise the content into a clear, logical flow.

**03 — Design**  
Build a restrained visual style that supports the message instead of competing with it.

## Principles

- **Clarity first** — every element has a purpose.
- **Consistency** — typography, spacing and layout work as one system.
- **Attention to detail** — clean alignment, readable hierarchy and thoughtful composition.
- **Made for the brief** — no one-size-fits-all templates.
